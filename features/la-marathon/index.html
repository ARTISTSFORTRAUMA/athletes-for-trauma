The Los Angeles Marathon is more than a race…

Race day · Century City · March 8, 2026

Story Tattiahna WelshPhotography & Design Per Bernal
…It’s a living symbol of perseverance, determination and the extraordinary strength powered by human spirit.

At the 2026 ASICS Los Angeles Marathon, Artists for Trauma was honored to celebrate the Wheelchair Division and para athletes in collaboration with The McCourt Foundation, California Rehabilitation Foundation, and California Rehabilitation Institute.

Together as a community, we gathered in Century City to support para athletes as they crossed the finish line and to celebrate their stories of resilience that extend far beyond the course.

The para athletesMore than 26.2 miles

For many of these para athletes, the marathon represents more than 26.2 miles. It means recovery after trauma, the courage to rebuild, and the refusal to let life-altering challenges define the future.

Every journey carries a story of adaptation, determination and transformation.

The volunteersFrom the start to the finish line

Throughout the day, volunteers from across the city came together from the start to the finish line, not only to celebrate athletic achievements, but to recognize something deeper: the courage it takes to continue moving forward through life’s greatest challenges.

Respect & appreciationSwag bags with para athletes

Together with our partners, we also shared thoughtfully curated swag bags with para athletes at the finish line, a small gesture of respect and appreciation made possible through the generosity and collaboration of our community organizations.

Our missionCollaborators united in support

Being there meant coming together as collaborators united in support. For Artists for Trauma, it was an opportunity to uplift the para athletes while reflecting our mission to foster healing, resilience, and connection through art and community.

The Los Angeles Marathon is a powerful reminder that resilience is not reserved for elite athletes. It lives in anyone who chooses to keep moving forward.

Century CityPowered by human spirit

At the finish line in Century City, surrounded by cheering crowds and exhausted but triumphant participants, that mission came to life. Each athlete who crossed the finish line embodied what it means to be Powered by Human Spirit.

Stay tuned as we continue sharing the voices and journeys behind these incredible athletes.
